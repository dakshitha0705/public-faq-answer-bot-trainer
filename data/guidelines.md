# Riverside City Services Department — Public Guidelines

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** "Riverside City Services Department" is an invented department created for a
> machine-learning demo. None of these rules, fees, addresses or phone numbers are real. Do not use them for any
> real-world decision.

## 1. Office Hours and Contact

- The main service centre is at 14 Harbor Street.
- The main service centre is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.
- On Saturdays the main service centre is open from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
- The main service centre is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- The general enquiries phone line is 555-0142 and is answered from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays.
- The online portal is available 24 hours a day.
- At the counter we accept cash and card. Cheques are not accepted.

## 2. Building Permits

- A residential building permit costs $150.
- A commercial building permit costs $400.
- Building permit applications are processed within 15 working days.
- You must submit a site plan, proof of ownership and your contractor licence number.
- A building permit is valid for 12 months.
- A building permit can be extended once for 6 months for a fee of $50.
- Garden sheds and other structures smaller than 10 square metres do not need a building permit.

## 3. Event Permits

- Apply for an event permit at least 30 days before the event.
- An event permit costs $75.
- Events with more than 500 people must submit a safety plan.

## 4. Waste Collection

- General waste is collected weekly: on Tuesday in the North district and on Thursday in the South district.
- Recycling is collected every two weeks.
- Garden waste in the green bin is collected on the first Monday of each month.
- Bins must be placed at the kerb by 6:00 AM on collection day and brought in by 8:00 PM.
- Bulky waste pickup must be booked online and costs $35 for up to 3 items.
- A replacement for a lost or stolen bin costs $40.
- A missed collection must be reported within 48 hours.

## 5. Water Bills

- Water bills are issued every 3 months.
- A water bill must be paid within 21 days of the bill date.
- A late payment fee of $15 is added to overdue water bills.
- Water bills can be paid on the online portal, by direct debit, or in person at the main service centre.
- Customers who pay by direct debit receive a 2% discount.
- Customers in financial hardship can request a payment plan of up to 6 months using form WB-7.
- Residents aged 65 or older receive a 20% concession on water bills.
- Water leaks and burst pipes should be reported to the 24-hour emergency line 555-0199.

## 6. Resident ID Cards

- A new resident ID card costs $25.
- A replacement for a lost resident ID card costs $30.
- A resident ID card must be renewed every 5 years, and renewal costs $20.
- You must bring photo ID and a proof of address dated within the last 3 months.
- Resident ID cards are ready within 10 working days and must be collected in person.
- Applicants under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

## 7. Parking Permits

- A resident parking permit costs $60 per year.
- Each household can hold a maximum of 2 resident parking permits.
- A book of 20 visitor day passes costs $10.

## 8. Dog Registration

- Dog registration costs $30 per year, or $15 per year for a desexed dog.
- Dog registration must be renewed by 31 March each year.
- A late registration fee of $10 applies after 31 March.

## 9. Complaints and Appeals

- The department responds to complaints within 10 working days.
- You can appeal a fine within 28 days of the fine date.
